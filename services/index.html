Our Practice

Every place carries its own rhythm.

AURAUM sessions are created around the space, the people within it, and the moment they are moving through.

Through sound, vibration and intention, we help bring greater clarity, ease and balance into the places where we live, work, gather, or any other space that may need a little love to open the door to a new chapter.

By helping to release stagnant energy, we create space for renewal, movement and new beginnings.
